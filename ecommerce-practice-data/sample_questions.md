# Sample Questions for VibeShop AI Assistant

Here is a list of questions to test different capabilities of your RAG Agent.

## 🔍 Product Discovery (Search)
*   "Show me some gaming laptops under $2000."
*   "Do you have any wireless earbuds with noise cancellation?"
*   "I need a camera for vlogging. What do you recommend?"
*   "Find me a smart thermostat for my home."
*   "What is the cheapest fitness tracker you sell?"

## 🆚 Product Comparison
*   "What is the difference between the Titan Edge and the AeroAir 13?"
*   "Compare the Lumix Vantage X1 and the SnapFocus lens."
*   "Which headphones have better battery life: ZenPods or SonicWave?"

## 📄 Policy & Support (RAG from Markdown)
*   "What is your return policy for open-box electronics?"
*   "How much does overnight shipping cost?"
*   "Do you ship to Canada?"
*   "My package is lost, what should I do?"
*   "Can I return a gift card?"

## 💡 Expert Advice (Reasoning)
*   "I am a college student studying graphic design. Which laptop should I buy?"
*   "I want to start a smart home setup. What products should I get first?"
*   "What is a good gift for someone who loves running?"
*   "Is the Titan Edge good for video editing?"

## 🧩 Complex/Multi-constraint
*   "Find me a laptop with at least 32GB RAM that is also good for gaming."
*   "I need a gift under $50 that works with Alexa."
*   "Show me light-weight cameras that shoot 4K video."
