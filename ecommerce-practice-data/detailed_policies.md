# VibeShop Comprehensive Policies

## Return & Exchange Policy

### Standard Return Policy
- **Return Window**: 30 days from delivery date for most items
- **Extended Return Window**: 60 days for VibeShop Premium members
- **Holiday Returns**: Items purchased between November 1st and December 31st can be returned until January 31st

### Electronics Return Policy
Electronics items have specific return conditions:
- **Laptops & Computers**: 15-day return window, must include all original accessories
- **Smartphones**: 14-day return window, device must be in original condition with less than 5% battery cycles
- **Gaming Equipment**: 30-day return window, software licenses are non-refundable
- **Opened Software**: Digital downloads and opened software cannot be returned

### Return Process
1. **Initiate Return**: Log into your account and select "Return Items" from your order history
2. **Print Return Label**: We provide prepaid return labels for items over $50
3. **Package Items**: Use original packaging when possible, include all accessories
4. **Drop Off**: Take to any authorized shipping location or schedule pickup
5. **Processing**: Returns are processed within 3-5 business days of receipt

### Refund Methods
- **Original Payment Method**: Full refunds processed to original payment method
- **Store Credit**: Optional store credit with 10% bonus value
- **Exchange**: Direct exchanges available for same-category items

## Shipping & Delivery Policy

### Standard Shipping Options
- **Economy Shipping**: 5-7 business days, FREE on orders over $35
- **Standard Shipping**: 3-5 business days, $5.99 flat rate
- **Express Shipping**: 2-3 business days, $12.99 flat rate
- **Overnight Shipping**: Next business day, $24.99 flat rate

### Premium Shipping Services
- **White Glove Delivery**: Available for large electronics, includes setup and installation
- **Scheduled Delivery**: Choose specific delivery time windows
- **Signature Required**: Available for high-value items over $500

### International Shipping
We ship to over 200 countries worldwide:
- **Canada**: 7-10 business days, duties and taxes calculated at checkout
- **Europe**: 10-14 business days, DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) available
- **Asia-Pacific**: 12-16 business days, customs forms included
- **Rest of World**: 14-21 business days, customer responsible for duties

### Shipping Restrictions
Certain items have shipping restrictions:
- **Lithium Batteries**: Cannot ship via air to certain destinations
- **Large Electronics**: May require freight shipping
- **Hazardous Materials**: Restricted shipping options available

## Price Match & Protection Policy

### Price Match Guarantee
We'll match competitor prices on identical items:
- **Eligible Retailers**: Major online and brick-and-mortar retailers
- **Identical Items**: Same model, brand, and specifications
- **In Stock**: Competitor must have item in stock
- **Exclusions**: Marketplace sellers, auction sites, membership clubs

### Price Protection
- **30-Day Protection**: If price drops within 30 days, we'll refund the difference
- **Automatic Monitoring**: We automatically track prices on your recent purchases

### Sale Price Matching
- **Current Sales**: We match current promotional prices
- **Coupon Matching**: We'll honor competitor coupons on identical items
- **Bundle Deals**: Price matching available on comparable bundles
- **Seasonal Sales**: Special consideration during major sale events
