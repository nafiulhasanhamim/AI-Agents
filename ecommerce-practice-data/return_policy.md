# Global E-commerce Return & Refund Policy

Last Updated: January 31, 2026

At **VibeShop**, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you're not, we're here to help.

## 1. Return Window
- **Standard Items**: You have **30 days** from the date of delivery to initiate a return.
- **Electronics**: Due to technical complexities, electronic items (cameras, laptops, tablets) must be returned within **14 days**.
- **Refurbished Items**: Returns accepted within **90 days** for defects only.

## 2. Condition of Returns
To be eligible for a full refund:
- Items must be in the **original packaging**.
- All accessories, manuals, and warranty cards must be included.
- For hygiene reasons, headphones and earbuds cannot be returned if the security seal is broken unless they are defective.

## 3. Refunds
- Once we receive your item, we will inspect it and notify you of the status of your refund.
- If approved, the refund will be initiated to your original payment method within **5-7 business days**.

## 4. Shipping Costs
- Returns are **FREE** if the item is defective or if we sent the wrong product.
- For "change of mind" returns, the customer is responsible for a flat **$9.99 return shipping fee**, which will be deducted from the refund.

## 5. Non-Returnable Items
- Digital gift cards.
- Downloadable software products.
- Personalized or custom-made items.
