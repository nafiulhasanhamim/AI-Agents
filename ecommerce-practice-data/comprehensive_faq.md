# VibeShop Comprehensive FAQ

## Ordering & Payment

### How do I place an order?
1. Browse our catalog and add items to your cart
2. Review your cart and apply any promo codes
3. Proceed to checkout and enter shipping information
4. Select payment method and complete your order
5. You'll receive an order confirmation email immediately

### What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major payment methods:
- **Credit Cards**: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover
- **Digital Wallets**: PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Amazon Pay
- **Buy Now, Pay Later**: Klarna, Afterpay, Affirm (for orders over $50)
- **Gift Cards**: VibeShop gift cards and store credit
- **Cryptocurrency**: Bitcoin, Ethereum (for orders over $100)

### Can I modify or cancel my order?
- **Before Processing**: Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of placement
- **During Processing**: Contact customer service immediately for possible changes
- **After Shipping**: Orders cannot be cancelled, but you can return items upon delivery
- **Digital Items**: Cannot be cancelled once download links are sent

### Do you offer financing options?
Yes, we partner with several financing providers:
- **Affirm**: 0% APR for 3-6 months on qualifying purchases
- **Klarna**: Pay in 4 installments with no interest
- **PayPal Credit**: Special financing offers for PayPal users
- **VibeShop Credit Card**: Exclusive financing and rewards program

## Shipping & Delivery

### How long does shipping take?
Shipping times vary by location and method:
- **Same Day**: Available in select metro areas for orders placed before 2 PM
- **Next Day**: Available nationwide for orders placed before 6 PM
- **2-3 Days**: Express shipping option
- **3-5 Days**: Standard shipping (free on orders over $35)
- **5-7 Days**: Economy shipping

### Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we ship to over 200 countries:
- **Canada & Mexico**: 5-10 business days
- **Europe**: 7-14 business days
- **Asia-Pacific**: 10-16 business days
- **Rest of World**: 14-21 business days
- **Duties & Taxes**: Calculated at checkout or paid upon delivery

### How can I track my order?
Multiple tracking options available:
- **Email Updates**: Automatic notifications at each shipping milestone
- **SMS Tracking**: Opt-in for text message updates
- **Account Dashboard**: Real-time tracking in your account
- **Mobile App**: Push notifications and detailed tracking
- **Carrier Tracking**: Direct links to UPS, FedEx, or USPS tracking

### What if my package is damaged or lost?
We take full responsibility for shipping issues:
- **Damaged Packages**: Photo documentation required, immediate replacement sent
- **Lost Packages**: Full investigation with carrier, replacement or refund provided
- **Stolen Packages**: Police report may be required, case-by-case resolution
- **Insurance**: All shipments over $100 are automatically insured

## Returns & Exchanges

### What is your return policy?
Our return policy is designed to be customer-friendly:
- **Standard Returns**: 30 days for most items
- **Electronics**: 15-30 days depending on category
- **Premium Members**: Extended 60-day return window
- **Holiday Returns**: Extended return period for holiday purchases

### How do I return an item?
Simple return process:
1. **Login**: Access your account and find the order
2. **Select Items**: Choose which items to return and reason
3. **Print Label**: Download and print prepaid return label
4. **Package**: Use original packaging when possible
5. **Ship**: Drop off at any authorized location or schedule pickup

### What items cannot be returned?
Certain items have return restrictions:
- **Digital Downloads**: Software, games, music, movies
- **Personalized Items**: Custom engraving, monogramming
- **Intimate Items**: Underwear, swimwear, personal care items
- **Perishables**: Food items, flowers, live plants
- **Final Sale Items**: Clearance items marked as final sale

### How long do refunds take?
Refund processing times:
- **Credit Cards**: 3-5 business days after we receive the return
- **PayPal**: 1-2 business days
- **Store Credit**: Immediate upon return processing
- **Original Payment Method**: Varies by bank processing times

## Troubleshooting

### Website Issues
Common website problems and solutions:
- **Slow Loading**: Clear browser cache, try different browser
- **Checkout Problems**: Disable ad blockers, check payment info
- **Search Issues**: Use different keywords, check spelling
- **Mobile Problems**: Update app, restart device
- **Account Access**: Password reset, clear cookies

### Order Issues
Resolving order problems:
- **Wrong Item Received**: Contact support immediately for exchange
- **Missing Items**: Check all packaging, report missing items within 48 hours
- **Damaged Items**: Photo documentation required for replacement
- **Billing Questions**: Review order confirmation, contact billing support
- **Delivery Issues**: Track package, contact carrier or our support team

### Product Issues
Getting help with products:
- **Setup Problems**: Consult manual, use our video guides, contact support
- **Performance Issues**: Check compatibility, update software/drivers
- **Warranty Claims**: Register product, document issue, contact manufacturer
- **Return Questions**: Review return policy, initiate return through account
- **Compatibility**: Use our compatibility checker or contact support
