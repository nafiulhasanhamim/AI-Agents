# Frequently Asked Questions: Shipping & Handling

### How long does shipping take?
We offer three tiers of shipping:
1. **Standard**: 5-7 business days ($4.99 or free over $50).
2. **Expedited**: 2-3 business days ($14.99).
3. **Priority Overnight**: Next-day delivery ($29.99).

### Do you ship internationally?
Currently, VibeShop ships to the **United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and Australia**. We are working on expanding to the EU later this year.

### Can I change my shipping address after an order is placed?
Address changes can be made within **1 hour** of order placement. After that, the item is moved to our logistics center and the address cannot be changed.

### What happens if my package is lost?
If your tracking hasn't updated in **10 days**, please contact our support team. We will investigate with the carrier and either issue a full refund or send a replacement at no extra cost.

### Why is my shipment delayed?
Delays are typically caused by:
- Inclement weather.
- Customs processing (for international orders).
- Incorrect address information.
- High peak seasons (Holidays).

### Tracking says delivered but I don't have it.
1. Check with neighbors or building managers.
2. Check "hidden" spots around your porch.
3. Wait 24 hours (sometimes carriers mark as delivered prematurely).
4. If still missing after 48 hours, file a claim with us.
